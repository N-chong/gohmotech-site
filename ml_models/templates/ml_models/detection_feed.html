How live detection works
  • The video stream is served by the system's camera engine. Detection runs on demand from this page: while it is open and the camera is connected, frames are sent to the server model every few seconds.
  • Each detection is saved and appears in Recent detections and the history page.
  • Closing this page stops detection. Continuous background detection would require a server-side worker and is noted as a future step.
